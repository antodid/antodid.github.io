Journey

From Utrecht to Rio to Rotterdam.

I’m doing an MSc in Finance & Investments at RSM in Rotterdam, after an Honours bachelor in Economics with a minor in Law at Utrecht.

This autumn I’m on IFSA Rotterdam’s private equity track, where my team is valuing TeamViewer for a take-private. I’m looking for private equity, venture capital or M&A internships for summer 2027.
